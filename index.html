About

More than a developer.

My background spans software development, systems engineering, infrastructure, technical support, AI, networking, and cybersecurity.

My work sits at the intersection of software and systems. I enjoy building applications, integrating hardware and software, deploying systems in constrained environments, and solving the problems that appear when all of those pieces have to work together.

I've worked across Linux and Windows environments, containerized applications, cloud and research infrastructure, networking, automation, and modern software development workflows. I enjoy understanding how a system works from the application layer down to the infrastructure supporting it.

My engineering experience includes developing an edge-optimized image codec with UDP-based data transmission, deploying applications through CloudLab and GitHub Actions, and developing a retrieval-augmented generation system that allows an LLM to work with information outside of its original training data.

I also bring a strong technical support foundation. Working directly with users, vendors, hardware, software, and networking issues taught me how to troubleshoot from both the technical and user perspectives and communicate solutions clearly.
